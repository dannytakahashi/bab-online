## Explainer engine (H1b; wired to /help by H2c below)

- `server/game/assist/`, pure and synchronous (no I/O, `Math.random`, `Date` or uuid; never makes a bot, never mutates `SEARCH`):
  - `seatView.js` `seatView(game, pos)`: the ONLY file that reads a GameState. A deep-frozen copy of what that seat may know: its own hand, trump, stage, `turnKey`, `currentTurn`, bids, score, the table, the contract (`BotController.buildPlayContext`) and the memory a bot in the seat would hold (`cardMemory.fromHistory` over `trickHistory` + `tableView.openTrick`), legal cards (play) or valid bids (bid). Null outside BIDDING / PLAYING. Keys pinned by `seatView.test.js`
  - `advise.js` `advise(view, {budget: 'full'|'rules', onError})`: exactly Mary's bid or card (`calculateOptimalBid` / `selectOptimalCard` with `personality 'mary'`, default search settings, a trace); `'rules'` passes `searchOptions {maxCards: 0}` (`source 'quick'` where the search would have run). A search error goes to `onError` (default: ignored), never to `SEARCH.errors` or the bots' log. Null when it is not the seat's turn
  - `explain.js` `explain(decision, view, {mode: 'help'|'tutorial', glossed})` -> `{headline, reasons (1-2), details (≤ 4), confidence, suggest, codes, gloss?}`, and `status(view)` (the off-turn line). Every reason comes from the path the bot took: the bid trace (EV curve, remove-one card attribution with `estimateOwnTricks`, make rates from `teamTrickDistribution` once the partner has bid), the terminal rule code and its facts, or the search's per-card `makes` / `sets` / `totals` over `samples` deals
  - `reasons.js`: the code -> words table (C.* contract line, L.* / F.* / D.* rule codes); `copy.js`: card and suit words, hedged rates ("usually (about 80%)", rounded to 5%), "about 1.9" tricks, "about +58" points, length caps (headline 60, reason 140, line 440: the plain-text line always carries both reasons, owner 2026-11-19), the glossary, `render(e, {style: 'rich'|'line', uses, freeUses, counted})`
- Copy rules (`copy.test.js` with `sim/lint.js` over every decision of seeded games): names only ("Bob (your partner)" on first mention, the other pair "Carol and Dan"; never team numbers, "your team", "opponents"), "suggested" and "our strongest bot", never "optimal", no gendered pronouns, no jargon (ruff, boss, EV), and no card the seat cannot see (own hand, played cards, the trump card)
- Tests: agreement with the Mary bot at every decision of 200 seeded games (`agreementA-D.test.js`, 50 games each, `testUtils/assistAgreement.js`; B-D only with `ASSIST_AGREEMENT_FULL=1`, about 2 min of CPU each: run them locally after any bot or assist change; A runs everywhere, CI included); fairness (`fairness.test.js`: a Proxy game that throws outside the read whitelist, 500 positions with the hidden hands dealt again giving byte-identical answers, 1,000 calls with Math.random / Date / uuid throwing, bot searches interleaved with /help); a real-handler game with /help at every turn for every seat identical to a plain one (`unperturbed.test.js`); `explain.test.js` (the owner-review fixtures, the plan's bid examples, forced / quick / status / glosses / onError); `perf.test.js` (/help p99 within the bot's own)
- Owner-review fixtures: `server/game/assist/__fixtures__/ownerReview.json` + `.md` (one decision per main reason code, the plan's two bid examples, a Quick suggestion, a tutorial answer, a status line). Rewrite both with `node server/game/assist/sim/buildFixtures.js` after a copy or bot change (`--check` says if stale); any bot-decision change also changes them
- Scripts (`server/game/assist/sim/`): `stateGame.js` (the sim loop on a real GameState, as the handlers keep it), `table.js` (a GameState at one decision), `assistCoverage.js --games N` (main reason codes, P.generic share, target < 2%, lint problems; exits 1 on either), `assistThreshold.js --games N --workers W --proxy mike|legacy` (the stats threshold sim: a weaker seat given Mary's choice on its top 2 / 3 EV-gap decisions per game, paired win-rate change)
- Threshold sim, 2026-11-18 (4,000 seat-balanced games per proxy, seed 1; oracle use of the free helps on the top EV-gap decisions, so an upper bound). Mike proxy (differs from Mary about 1.3 times a game): 2 helps +3.2 pp pair win rate (±1.0), 3 helps +3.1 pp (±1.0): a third gap rarely exists. Legacy-Mary proxy (differs about 23 times a game): 2 helps +6.3 pp (±1.1), 3 helps +8.1 pp (±1.2). The owner kept 3 free uses (2026-11-18); change it only with the owner

## /help on the server (H2c)

- Entry: `chatMessage` only (no new c2s event). `/help` (also `/help <words>`; `chatHandlers.ownerCommand`, before the human / bot branches) and the exact line `?help` (any case, surrounding spaces ignored; `chatHandlers.chatMessage`, before the profanity filter and the log: the only way shipped iOS builds 6-14 can ask, since they refuse unknown `/` commands on the device). Neither is ever broadcast, filtered or stored as chat; "?help me", "help!" and "?helpme" stay chat. A spectator gets "/help is for players only." unless its recorded user owns a seat here (`chatHandlers.recordedSeat`, the /active lookup): then "A bot is playing your seat. Type /active first." when a bot holds the away seat, else the /active branch's line ("You left this game...", "Your seat is controlled from another connection...", "Reconnect to take your seat back."); an entrant (countdown, draw) "Suggestions are available once the game starts.", for both forms. Lines that tell a player to wait or retry never name /help (shipped iOS can only send ?help). No tutorial pieces yet (no /tutorial, ?tutorial, users.tutorial, auto-start or tips: a later PR after deploy survival PR 5)
- `server/socket/assistHandlers.js` `help(socket, io, game, pos)`, synchronous; guards, each a private reply never counted or announced, in order: a bot-controlled seat (lazy or resigned, either view) "A bot is playing your seat. Type /active first."; `ASSIST_IN_TOURNAMENTS` (true, owner 2026-11-18: allowed at tournament tables and counted there; false would answer "/help isn't available in tournament games."); the rate limit `rateLimiter` key `assist` {max 4, windowMs 10000} per socket (after the wrapper's chatMessage limit) "One moment: try again in a few seconds."; no open turn (`!acceptsTurnInput()`, no SeatView, advise null: the seat already bid) "Nothing to suggest right now."; off turn the status line (`explain.status`, e.g. "Not your turn yet: Carol is bidding. Bob (your partner) bid 2.")
- The answer: `seatView` -> `advise` -> `explain(mode 'help')`, cached per game in a module `WeakMap` keyed `${pos}|${game.turnKey()}` (at most `CACHE_PER_GAME` 8 per game, oldest out), so a re-ask is free. A play the search would look at (`advise.searchEligible`) takes a token of a process-wide bucket, `BUDGET_PER_SEC` 60 searched answers a second refilling continuously (`createBudget({perSec, now})`); without one it is rules only (`budget 'rules'`, confidence `quick`, "Quick suggestion, rules only (only you see this): ...", never cached). Bids and rule-only plays spend nothing. The search's own errors go to a local `onError` (warn '/help search error' with `cardsInHand` / `handSize` / the error's name: never a card), never `SEARCH.errors` or `noteSearchError`. Anything that throws: "No suggestion this time.", not counted, error '/help failed'. `/health` `assist` {answered, quick, errors} since boot
- Counting (`game.assist[pos]`, `server/game/assistState.js`, SAVED by codec v2): a forced move (one legal card) is answered ("... (Not counted.)") but not counted or announced; a re-ask on the turn already counted (`lastHelpKey === turnKey`, also after a restore) is answered from the cache, not counted. Before counting, a record whose `user` is not the seat's `ownerUsername` is reset (the defensive reset). The counter moves BEFORE the notice's `addLogEntry`, whose persist hook saves the game. `ASSIST_FREE_HELP` 3 (assistState; change only with the owner): `isAssistedSeat(a, owner)` = `a.user === owner && a.help > 3`
- Delivery (plan 4.3): a socket with the clientHello cap `assist` (`clientInfo.KNOWN_CAPS`, `$defs.clientCapAssist`; web sends it from 2026-11-19 (H3, below), iOS from b15 (H4)) gets the private s2c `assistHint` {v 1, source 'help', kind bid | card | status, turnKey, headline, reasons ≤ 2, uses, freeUses, assisted, counted, details ≤ 4, confidence, suggest {bid} | {card}, codes, note?}; refusals and the status line are kind `status` with the whole reply in `headline`. Any other socket gets private, unsequenced, NEVER stored `gameLogEntry {message, type: 'system'}` lines (`copy.render` 'line', ≤ 440 characters (both reasons always fit), ending "(Help 2 of 3 free)", "(Help 4: this game counts as bot-assisted for you)" or "(Not counted.)"), at most 2 per ask: the second only on the last free use, the warning "That's your 3rd free /help. The next one makes this game count as bot-assisted for you: it goes in your bot-game stats, not the human leaderboard." (with the cap it rides in `note`; on the crossing use `note` is "This game now counts as bot-assisted for you."). A rejoin loses them (asking again on the same turn is free)
- Snapshot (2026-11-19, additive): snapshot v2 `viewer.assist {uses, freeUses, assisted}` = the receiving seat owner's own count (`assistState.assistTally(game.assist[pos], ownerUsername)`, the same function the hint's uses / freeUses / assisted come from; `snapshotV2.js`), present for role seat and lazyWatcher, absent for spectators, entrants and nobody. Clients use it for the count after a reload (web: the chat autocomplete). Test: `assistHandlers.test.js` "snapshot viewer.assist"
- The notice, every counted use (so at most once per turn): the stored, broadcast room line "<name> used /help.", on the crossing use "<name> used /help. This game now counts as bot-assisted for <name>." (the owner's username; never a team number, "your team" or "opponents")
- Game end (`gameHandlers.handleHandComplete`, before `terminate`/gameEnd): each assisted player's owner socket gets the private line "This game counted as bot-assisted for you (4 /help uses), so it's in your bot-game stats." (`assistHandlers.gameEndLines`)
- Stats writer (`profileHandlers.buildGameEndRecord`, plan section 6): an assisted seat gets `statsDeltas.bucketByPosition {<pos>: 'botStats'}` when the whole-game bucket is 'stats' (the H2a reader, deployed before this writer, credits it), the record `withBots: true` (+ empty `teamNBots` for human seats: the game's team records move to the "with bots" Records scope, plan Q4) and `assistedTeams: [1|2]` (server-only, in no Records projection); every seat with counted uses is in `statsDeltas.assist {<pos>: {help}}`. A game nobody used /help in gives the record it gave before, byte for byte (`assistRecords.test.js`). Lazy seats stay human (unchanged)
- Bid clock (`server/socket/bidClock.js`): /help never pauses, stops, resets or delays it (it touches no timer and emits no bidClock); its timeout bid of 0 is not a /help use
- Tests: `server/socket/__tests__/assistHandlers.test.js` (cap vs no-cap delivery, private lines in no snapshot, notice stored and named, re-ask, forced, status, refusals, rate limit, 3rd warns / 4th crosses with and without the cap, the counter before the persist hook, the defensive reset, ?help exact match, spectators and entrants, the budget and the bucket, both error paths, the bid clock, the game-end line), `assistRecords.test.js` (the writer, the reader crediting, a real tournament: allowed, counted, assisted), `server/game/persist/__tests__/assistRestore.test.js` (kill and restore after 3 uses: the re-ask on the restored turn is free, the 4th crosses and says so once), `contract.test.js` "(b3) /help" (assistHint and both gameLogEntry forms observed; seq and snapshot invariants)

## /help on the web (H3, 2026-11-19)

- Cap: `utils/avatar.js clientHello` always sends `assist`, so this page's /help is answered with s2c `assistHint`, never the private plain-text lines. `handlers/gameHandlers.js` registers it (unsequenced, `socketManager.on`; passed through `handlers/index.js` as `onAssistHint`); main.js hands it to `GameScene.handleAssistHint` through the director (table order) while at the table; on the game-end screen a status reply ("Nothing to suggest right now.") is written as its private row (`assistHintRoute`: 'scene' / 'row' / null)
- Words and rules: `client/src/state/assistHint.js` (pure). `hintIsLive(hint, state, {pending})`: a bid/card hint shows only on my own turn at my seat (not lazy, not a spectator), nothing of mine pending, and its turnKey's stage, seat, (bids) bid count and (cards) cardIndex match GameState (`clientCardIndex` = (trickNo - 1) * 4 + the cards in the trick, skipped while trickNo is unknown). dealSeq is not compared: the client's count is its own (+1 per gameStart, null after a v1 rejoin), and a mismatch would drop every hint for the rest of the game; a status reply always shows (spectators' "players only", the lazy seat's "Type /active first"). The confidence labels mirror `copy.js CONFIDENCE_LABEL`; the footer is "What our strongest bot would play · Help 2 of 3 free" / "· Help 4: this game counts as bot-assisted for you" / "· Asked again this turn: not counted", "Not counted" for a forced move, the Quick note for `quick`
- This game's uses (`resetAssistUses` on createUI, `noteAssistUses` from every non-spectator hint and from every snapshot's `viewer.assist` (`state/tableSync.applyTableSnapshot`, 2026-11-19), `forgetAssistUses` on the table teardown) drive the chat autocomplete: `/help` is first in a seat's list (not a pure spectator's) with "Suggest a move (n of 3 free used)", at 3 "Suggest a move (the next one makes this game bot-assisted)", past it "Suggest a move (this game is bot-assisted for you)", and "Suggest a move (after 3 free, this game counts as bot-assisted)" only while unknown (no hint and no snapshot with `viewer.assist` yet, e.g. an older server; the warning stays, since the free uses may be spent).
