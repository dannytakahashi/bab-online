# Spectator badge and spectator chat

Never auto-loaded: open it for anything about who is watching a table (server roster, wire, web badge and bubble, iOS 1.2.0). Membership of `playerGames` vs spectators is in `server/CLAUDE.md`, Seats and presence; the badge geometry is in `docs/claude/web-table.md`.

- Spectator badge (owner, 2026-11-11: "who is spectating", seen by everyone at the table): `GameState.spectatorList()` is the ONE membership rule: pure spectators (`game.spectators`), one per username in join order, never a seat owner (a lazy, resigned or /leave'd owner watching from another socket or tab, a demoted older socket) or a pre-seating entrant. `server/game/spectatorRoster.js`: `GameManager.addSpectator` / `leaveSpectating` (every join, leave, spectate-another-game, round start, reclaim, watchSeat demotion; `handleDisconnect` too, with the io the game was last scheduled with) call `spectatorsChanged(io, game)`, which coalesces on the game's own `spectators` timer (250 ms; nothing after end/abort) and sends `spectatorsUpdated {gameId, spectators: [{username, pic, avatarUrl?}], count}` to the room when the list changed (it also sends when the list only differed inside the window, e.g. a join and leave: a snapshot in between showed the joiner); UNSEQUENCED, because the App Store build (f05d264) has no handler and a sequenced form would open a gap in its seq gate on every change. Pictures: `lookupPic` reads the saved picture after the join (projected findOne, no random fallback, no RNG) and the list updates when it lands; `server/game/spectatorWire.js` sends a stock number or null, a custom picture only as `avatarUrl`; capped at 50, `count` the true total. Snapshot v2 `watching` carries the same {spectators, count} (a reload shows it at once). The older `spectatorCount` fields (notice, full state, snapshot, inProgressGames) keep cc865b4's meaning, the spectator SOCKET count (wireCompat pins them; ambient demand reads `game.spectators.size`). Web: GameState `applySpectators` / `applySnapshot` (`parseWatching`) / `renderModel().watching` (a new gameId and reset() clear it), `ui/components/SpectatorBadge.js` (kit IconButton as a disclosure (aria-expanded/aria-controls, no aria-haspopup; the list is role=list), drawn eye SVG + count, hidden at 0, aria-label "2 people watching"; click/Enter or a mouse hover opens the names with avatars, Escape / outside click closes (Escape refocuses the badge only when focus was in it, or nowhere after a click; a hover-opened list leaves the chat input's focus alone); fades in (style flushed before `.is-open`), closes at once; reduced motion: no fade) drawn by main.js `renderTable` and the event handler, removed in `teardownTableUI`; the "X joined as a spectator" log line stays. A spectator's chat (owner, 2026-11-11: "a similar chat bubble ... above the eye with the spectators name"): main.js onChatMessage sends every `isSpectator` message (a pure spectator's, and a watching owner's, which the server also sends without a position; a seat-view lazy owner and players keep their seat) to `ChatBubble.showSpectatorChatBubble` ("Zach: hello", the seats' 3-line cap and 6 s, `.is-spectator` tint from the log's `--success`, one at a time; its own HUD slot `.hud-spectator-bubble`, hung from the layout's `spectators.bubble` spot (chosen after the seats' bubbles: above the badge first, slid along it, then beside it, the BUBBLE_WIDTHS steps; never over BUBBLE_KEEPS, my box, the voice buttons, the pill or the bid sheet: `badgeBubbleKeeps`; where the badge's first spot has no room it takes its next one, and where none has (a phone-size player, an 880x500 window) there is no spot and the slot hides: a transient bubble never shrinks the table); it shows at the laid-out spot even while the badge is still hidden (a spectator who chats before the coalesced list), keeps through a render/relayout, goes with clearChatBubbles/clearHud; no live region, as the seats').
