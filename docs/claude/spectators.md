Never auto-loaded: open it for anything about who is watching a table (server roster, wire, web badge and bubble, iOS 1.2.0) and for the over-the-shoulder view (a spectator watching one seat's hand: last section). ## Over-the-shoulder view

Owner decisions 2026-11-21. Server phase 1 is `server/socket/shoulderView.js` (+ the deal loop in `gameHandlers.dealHand`, `snapshotHandlers.snapshotFor`, `spectatorRoster.flush`, the hint mirror in `assistHandlers`). The web sends `watchHand` from 2026-11-21 (phase 2, "Web" below); the iOS phase builds on this section, which is its spec. Contract: c2s `watchHand` (status `active`, `sentBy` web), s2c `watchHandRefused` (listed for the web, `WATCH_HAND` / `WATCH_HAND_REFUSED`; both still excluded from the generated Swift constants until the iOS phase lists them in `contract/layout.json`), optional keys `stateSnapshot.reason: 'watchHand'`, `$defs.viewerV2.shoulder` / `$defs.shoulderHand` (generated Swift `TableSnapshot.Viewer.shoulder: ShoulderHand?`, `Spectator.shoulder: Int?`), the spectator `gameStart.shoulder`, `assistHint.watched`, `$defs.spectatorEntry.shoulder`; cap `$defs.clientCapShoulderView`. One of each payload, from a real game: `contract/fixtures/shoulder/events.json` (`npm run contract:fixtures`; deliberately not in `contract/fixtures/v2`, whose web and iOS suites iterate and count it).

- What it is: a pure spectator (in `game.spectators`, never a seat owner, lazy watcher or entrant) may watch one seat's hand, read-only: the table that seat sees (its hand, its HSI, its bid clock and bid bubble, which are public already, and its /help and tutorial cards as they come), and switch seats by tapping a player's avatar. Everyone who spectates may still talk and chat as today. Stats are unaffected by being watched.
- The cap: only a socket whose last `clientHello` has `shoulderView` may use it, and only such a socket ever receives any of its data. Every other socket's `gameStart`, snapshots and `spectatorsUpdated` are byte-identical to before (shipped builds receive nothing new), and a `watchHand` from a socket without the cap is dropped silently (debug log; no reply, not even a refusal). A later hello without the cap ends the socket's watch at once.
- Fairness rules (owner): (1) never at a tournament table (refused live; the table knows it itself, `game.tournamentId`, set when the round creates it and at hydrate, so a table whose tournament was cancelled or deleted while it still lives, e.g. a cancel while it was suspended, stays one; its saved doc keeps the tournamentId too, since `gameCodec.serialize` prefers the game's mark to the map, so an adopter takes it with its tournament or discards it as 'no tournament', never as a casual table); (2) the own-seat rule: a spectating socket whose username owns a seat at this table, gone seats included (a player's second tab, a demoted older socket, a /leave'd owner watching from a new socket; the spectator entry's recorded name and the signed-in name are both checked), may watch only that seat; (3) transparency: the table sees who watches which hand (`shoulder` on the spectator list's entries, below).
- c2s `watchHand {gameId, position: 1-4 | null, requestId?}` (Joi `watchHand`; `null` stops watching; `requestId` a string of 1-64 or an integer, echoed). Rate limit `watchHand` 20 per 30 s per socket (one per avatar tap); over it the wrapper's `serverError {code: 'rateLimit', handler: 'watchHand'}`; a malformed payload (position 0, 5, '2', missing; a bad gameId) is `serverError {code: 'validation'}`. Neither changes the watch.
- Accepted: the server stores `game.shoulders.set(socketId, position)` (or deletes it for null) and replies at once, on that socket only, `stateSnapshot {snapshot, reason: 'watchHand', requestId?}`: a full v2 snapshot with the log left out (`partial: ['log']`: keep your log) whose `viewer` is unchanged (`role: 'spectator'`, `position: null`, `canAct: false`; NEVER the watched seat: shipped builds rotate the table by viewer.position) plus `viewer.shoulder {position, hand, hsi}` while watching (absent after a stop). `hand` is that seat's cards now (the ones it already played are gone), `hsi` that seat's HSI of this deal (a number; null only if none was computed). Top-level `hand` and `hsi` stay null (a spectator's). Re-sending the same position is accepted and answered the same way (an idempotent resync). Then, within 250 ms, the coalesced `spectatorsUpdated` (below). Every later snapshot this socket gets while watching (requestSnapshot replies, a spectatorJoined full state) carries `viewer.shoulder` too; `reason` is on the watchHand reply only.
- Refused: `watchHandRefused {gameId, position, reason, message, ownPosition?}` on that socket only; nothing changes (a seat already watched stays watched, e.g. a refused switch). Reasons, checked in this order: `not_spectating` (not a pure spectator of that gameId: another or unknown game, a seat owner, a lazy watcher, an entrant), `unavailable` (the table is suspended by the LIVE_PERSIST self-fence, frozen by a handoff, or over: nothing about a hand goes out of such a table on any path, the direct snapshot replies included; try again after "Game resumed."), `tournament`, `not_dealt` (before the first gameStart: CREATED, the draw, the teams; a client sends watchHand after its first gameStart), `own_seat` (with `ownPosition`: offer only that seat, or send watchHand(ownPosition)). A stop (null) is refused only for `not_spectating`: a stop exposes nothing, so it is always done, and on a suspended, frozen or ended table it gets no reply at all (no snapshot leaves such a table; the client has already dropped its shoulder UI on its own stop). One rule set (`shoulderView.refusal`) also decides every snapshot's `viewer.shoulder`, every deal's `gameStart.shoulder` and every mirrored hint, so while a table is suspended or frozen a watch is kept but serves nothing. `message` is short English a client may show (`shoulderView.REFUSALS`).
- Every deal (each hand and every all-zero redeal: `destroyHands` then a fresh deal): the watcher's own spectator `gameStart` (the same seq as the deal's other gameStarts; order unchanged: seat gameStarts, then spectator gameStarts, then on the first hand the 1 s pause, then `updateTurn`) carries `shoulder {position, hand, hsi}`: the watched seat's new hand and this deal's HSI for that seat only. `hand: []` stays the spectator variant's top-level hand. A spectator without the cap or not watching gets no `shoulder` key.
- Cards leaving the hand: no new event. `cardPlayed {position, card}` is public: when `position` is the watched seat, remove that card from the shoulder hand (the lazy watcher path does the same for its own hand). The next `gameStart.shoulder` is the new hand. Any snapshot's `viewer.shoulder` replaces the hand wholesale.
- HSI: a watcher gets only the watched seat's HSI (`shoulder.hsi`), never any other seat's: spectator gameStarts carry no `hsiValues` and spectator snapshots `hsi: null`. Seat owners receive only their own seat's HSI (#362); the Jest-wide `server/testUtils/hsiGuard.js` allows the one shoulder exception (that seat's HSI under `shoulder`, to a pure spectator whose watch is that seat, with the game's value) and fails anything else, and `shoulderCheck.js` (`ownerHsi: 'own'`) checks the same in the shoulder suites.
- The watched seat's hints (`assistHint` with `watched: <position>`): each hint the watched seat's owner socket gets is copied to the seat's watchers right after it, whatever the owner's own client renders (its `assist` / `tutorial` caps): its /help answers (`source: 'help'`, kind bid / card, counted or not, forced moves too), its tutorial advice (`source: 'tutorial'`, kind bid / card) and its rule tips (kind `tip`). NOT mirrored: kind `status` (refusals and status lines are the asker's), the system tips `T.welcome`, `T.graduate` and `T.done` (they speak to the player about their tutorial), the private plain-text lines, `assistState`. The copy drops `note` (the last-free-use warning, the crossing) and `actions` (Keep / Turn off); `uses`, `freeUses`, `assisted`, `counted` and `unlimited` stay but describe the WATCHED player: show no count on a watched hint. Only as they come: nothing is replayed to a new watcher or after a switch. Clear a watched hint on the same events the owner's client does, from the watched seat's view: the next `updateTurn`, the watched seat's own `bidReceived` / `cardPlayed`, hand end, a turnKey that is not the current turn's, and a switch or stop. Watchers never ask: a watcher's own /help is still answered "for players only" (kind status, no `watched`).
- Bid clock and bid bubble: no new data. `bidClock` and `bidReceived` go to the whole room; while the watched seat is the bidder show its countdown (`bidClock.position === shoulder.position`) the way the bidder sees it, and its bid bubble as for any seat. The bid screen (owner, changed 2026-11-21): a READ-ONLY mirrored bid sheet on the watched seat's bid turn, where a seated player's goes: that seat's legal choices from public state (hand size, the bores so far), the mirrored hint's ring, the countdown; no selection, nothing that sends. The player's in-progress selection is never on the wire and is never mirrored.
- Who watches which hand: while anybody watches, every socket of the table with the cap (its pure spectators and its seat owners' sockets) gets `spectatorsUpdated` with `shoulder: <position>` on the watchers' entries, privately, right after the room's plain copy (when the plain list changed too) or alone (when only who watches which hand changed); both are the whole list, so apply the last one received. When the last watch ends the capable sockets get the list without `shoulder`. A capable viewer's snapshots carry the same `shoulder` keys in `watching.spectators`. One entry per username (the list's rule): a person watching from two sockets shows the first one's seat. A seat owner's own second tab is never in the list (spectatorList skips seat owners), so its watch of its own seat is not listed. Render read-only, e.g. "Zach is watching Alice's hand" on the badge row; label people by name (never seat or team numbers).
- Paused tables (client rule): a table suspended by the self-fence keeps its watches but serves nothing until it thaws, so while it is paused (an `unavailable` refusal, or between the room's system lines "Game paused: the server is reconnecting. It will resume on its own." and "Game resumed.") a snapshot without `viewer.shoulder` does NOT end the watch: keep the selected seat, show the watched hand area as paused (no stale cards), and send `watchHand(selected)` again after "Game resumed." and after the next full state (`spectatorJoined` / `rejoin` carriers, a requestSnapshot reply); the reply restores `viewer.shoulder`. A table that ends instead sends `abortGame` / `gameEnd` as usual (drop the selection then). Only the person's own stop or one of the endings below drops the selection.
- A watch ends (the server forgets it; `game.shoulders` is DERIVED, never saved): `/leave` while spectating, `joinMainRoom`, spectating another game (re-spectating the SAME game from the same socket keeps it: its spectatorJoined full state then carries `viewer.shoulder`), the socket's disconnect, a hello without the cap, the socket becoming a seat owner (`/active` reclaimSeat, `rejoinGame`, joinAsSpectator onto its own lazy seat), the game's end or abort (retire). No event announces it: outside a paused table (previous bullet) a client drops its shoulder UI on any snapshot carrier without `viewer.shoulder` (or with a role other than spectator), on leaving the table, and after its own stop.
- Re-send rule: a watch belongs to one socket's spectating. After a `spectatorJoined` full state WITHOUT `viewer.shoulder` (a reconnect on a new socket, spectating another game and coming back, a server restart: a deploy-survival adoption brings back no spectators and no watches, so watchers re-spectate and their full state has none) a client that wants the view sends `watchHand` again; re-sending is idempotent (the same position is answered with the same reply), so a client may simply re-send after every full state. A foreground probe that kept the socket keeps the watch.
- What clients render: the watched seat's hand face up where my hand would be (the client may rotate its own drawing so the watched seat is at the bottom; the wire never does), never selectable or playable (no bid or card input, no lightbulb, no ask), the watched seat's HSI, its mirrored hint cards (read-only: no Keep / Turn off, nothing that sends), the clock pill and bid bubble; switching by tapping another player's avatar (`watchHand(position)`), stopping by tapping the watched one again or a "Stop watching" control (`watchHand(null)`); at tournament tables (snapshot `tournament` non-null) no avatar tap; with a recorded seat (`own_seat`) only that seat. Shipped builds keep today's spectator view.
- Tests: `server/socket/__tests__/shoulderView.test.js` (accepted, every refusal, switching, stopping, every deal, cardPlayed, mirrored /help and tutorial hints, the wide list, every way a watch ends), `shoulderChaos.test.js` (60 chaos seeds, `SHOULDER_SEEDS`, with random caps and watchHand from any socket), `server/game/persist/__tests__/shoulderRestore.test.js` (serialize and the write store: nothing saved, a re-sent watchHand after adoption), `contract.test.js` (b5), `contractFixtures.test.js` (`contract/fixtures/shoulder/events.json`); every one runs the live emit scan `server/testUtils/shoulderCheck.js` (no socket without the cap gets shoulder data, a watcher only its seat's hand / HSI / hints, no seat owner another seat's hand, never at a tournament table or against the own-seat rule, no stale `game.shoulders` entry).
